The research is done and `REPORT.md` is written. This milestone turns that
result into something a person outside the lab can read: a single,
self-contained results site, built from the repo's own data and published
with GitHub Pages.

`smol.*` module. The brief is `results-site-brief.md`, not `BRIEFING.md`,
and where the two disagree about what this work is, the site brief wins.
`BRIEFING.md` still governs everything the lab measured.

The lab stays closed as research. The repository is open again for
publishing.
